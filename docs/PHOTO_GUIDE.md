# Photographing the jewellery — a phone guide

The catalogue looks premium only when the photographs are consistent. One
15-minute session with a phone and a window beats a hundred mixed snapshots.

## What you need

- A phone with a clean lens (wipe it with a soft cloth every time — most blurry
  jewellery photos are just a fingerprint).
- Daylight near a large window. **No direct sun**, no overhead bulb, no ring
  light. Between 10 am and 4 pm is easiest.
- A plain background: white or deep maroon cloth with no creases, or a smooth
  ivory sheet. One colour for the whole catalogue.
- A small stand, box or book to raise the piece to eye level. No hands in frame.
- A soft brush and a lint-free cloth to remove dust and fingerprints.

## Each piece — 4 photos and 1 video

1. **Front**, straight on, the whole piece, small margin around it.
2. **Angle** (about 45°) to show depth and setting.
3. **Detail** — clasp, hallmark stamp, stone setting, or the motif.
4. **On a neutral surface** (worn on a hand/neck only if the model is
   comfortable and the piece is clean).
5. **Video, 10–15 seconds**: turn the piece slowly once on the stand under the
   same light. No zooming, no filters, no music.

Keep the same distance and framing for every piece so the grid looks calm.

## Settings and editing

- Use the main (1×) lens, tap to focus on the metal, then lower exposure slightly
  until the highlights stop glowing white.
- Turn **off** HDR, flash, beauty mode and any filter.
- Do not sharpen, saturate or add vignettes. Cropping to a consistent 4:5 is the
  only edit needed.
- Export at about **1600 px on the long edge, WebP or high-quality JPEG**. A
  20 MB photo is not better; it is just slower on a phone.

## File names

Use the item code so a photo can never be attached to the wrong piece:

```
TJ-GR-014-front.webp
TJ-GR-014-angle.webp
TJ-GR-014-detail.webp
TJ-GR-014-worn.webp
TJ-GR-014-video.mp4
```

Lower case, hyphens, no spaces, no dates in the name.

## Uploading

1. Admin → Products → the item (or create it first with its code).
2. Add the photos in order: front, angle, detail, worn. The first one is the card
   image.
3. **Alt text is mandatory** — describe the piece for someone who cannot see it,
   e.g. "22K gold temple necklace with ruby cabochons". Do not write "image" or
   the file name.
4. Add the video last (MP4/WebM, under about 20 MB). It plays muted on hover on
   desktop and on tap on mobile.
5. Publish only when the photo, weight, purity and availability are all real.

## What never to upload

- Photos or videos from another jeweller's website, Instagram, Google Images or
  a supplier catalogue you do not have written permission to use.
- Stock images presented as pieces in stock. The only stock photography on this
  site is the labelled "Design inspiration" strip, credited in
  [public/images/CREDITS.md](../public/images/CREDITS.md).
- Photos of a piece you cannot currently show or order. Use the empty state
  instead — it is honest and it converts better than a broken promise.
