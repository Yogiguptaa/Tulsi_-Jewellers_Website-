# Image credits

Every photograph and short video shipped with this site, with the licence and the
page it came from. The storefront reads `public/media/manifest.json`; this file is
the human record, and [/image-credits](/image-credits) renders the same table.

**Design inspiration only.** A stock photo shows a style, never an item in stock,
and never carries a price. The showroom's own photographs replace these files as
they are uploaded (see [docs/PHOTO_GUIDE.md](../../docs/PHOTO_GUIDE.md)); the
manifest entry and its row here are then deleted together.
